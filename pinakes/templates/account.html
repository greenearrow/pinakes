A token lets a program on one of your machines (the pinakes command, a script) ask the catalog as you: only what you may see, never stepped up. Make one per machine, so you can revoke one alone. Every call it makes is listed on Access to your records.{% if my_groups %} A group token asks as one of your groups instead: it sees what the group owns or has shared with it, and none of your own or any other member's files; every member sees its calls and may revoke it, and it stops working if you leave the group.{% endif %}
